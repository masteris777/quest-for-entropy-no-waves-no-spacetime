**Series:** ← [#14 The Poor Cat Experiments](https://github.com/masteris777/quest-for-entropy-the-poor-cat-experiments) · [#16 Spooky Action on the Ledger](https://github.com/masteris777/quest-for-entropy-spooky-action-on-the-ledger) →

The article **"No Waves, No Spacetime"** (Quest for Entropy #15), archived as published,
with its assets. Spacetime as something that happens to waves - and only to waves - in a
computational universe with a fixed compute budget: gravity as a crowd, time as a budget,
and at the bottom of the deepest crowd a black hole with no singularity anywhere, plus
three pre-registered thermodynamics tests that fail and are published red.

The model itself - the paper, the code, and the full examination battery (including the
test rows this article cites) - lives in its own repository:
https://github.com/questforentropy/iceberg-model (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22046196).
Reproduction instructions are there, not here.

## What is in here

- `article.md` - the piece as published
- `appendix-the-red-rows.md` - a diagnosis of the three failed thermodynamics tests
  and the scout that shows the shape of the repair (an existence proof, not a fix)
- `assets/` - the article's images and animations
- `sim/make_figures.py` - regenerates the cartoon animations
  (`python sim/make_figures.py`; needs numpy, matplotlib, pillow). A cartoon of the
  mechanism, not the certified model - the article says so too.
- `sim/sg_river_scout.py` - the appendix's scout (`python sim/sg_river_scout.py`;
  needs numpy only)
